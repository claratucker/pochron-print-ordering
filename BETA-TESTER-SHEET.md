# Pochron Studios Print Ordering — Beta Tester Guide

Thanks for helping test the new online print ordering tool. It's a full working
version, but it's in **test mode**, so a few things are worth knowing before you
start.

## What to know first

- **No real charges.** Payments run in test mode. You must use the **test card
  below** — a real card will (correctly) be declined and is never charged.
- The tool should be self-explanatory: upload photos, pick paper and size,
  optionally edit or crop, then check out.

## Placing a test order

1. Go to **https://order.pochronstudios.com/order/mockupV5.html**
2. Upload one or more photos, choose paper and size, and (optionally) edit/crop.
3. At checkout, enter shipping details and pay with the **test card**:

   > **Card number:** `4242 4242 4242 4242`
   > **Expiry:** any future date (e.g. `12 / 34`)
   > **CVC:** any 3 digits (e.g. `123`)
   > **ZIP:** any (e.g. `10001`)

4. You should reach an order confirmation.

## What we'd love you to try

- Different photo sizes and both border options (white border vs. full bleed).
- The "Edit color & crop" editor, and the "Hand Color Correction" option.
- A large or high-resolution file, and a small/low-res one (to see the warning).
- Anything that feels confusing, slow, or broken.

## Known during beta

- Payments are test-mode only (the card above).
- You're placing real-looking orders, but nothing ships and no card is charged.
