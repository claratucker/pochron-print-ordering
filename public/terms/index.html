Ordering and review

You upload your images, choose paper, size, and finishing, and submit your order. Every order is reviewed by Pochron Studios before it is printed. If we see a problem, such as a file that is too low-resolution for the size you picked, or anything that looks unintended, we will contact you before printing.

Color, editing, and proofs

You can edit color and crop your image yourself, or add Hand Color Correction, in which case Pochron Studios prepares the color from your file before printing. Screens are not print. On-screen color depends on your monitor and may differ from the final print. If you want us to prepare the color, choose Hand Color Correction. For exhibition work or more involved editing, contact the studio.
